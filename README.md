# Dad Joke Showdown

One dad joke, at most 200 characters — that joke is the whole entry.
A judge model reads two jokes at a time and picks the one that lands
the bigger groan.

Entry for the [Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The joke

> My son asked why I always say 'Hi hungry, I'm Dad.' I told him it's not a joke, it's a lifestyle.

97 characters. A fresh twist on the oldest gag in the book, delivered
deadpan at the dinner table.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes the joke to /output/entry.txt
```
